Process

From art to finished pieces

1 · Art

Send your logo. Vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) work best.

2 · Digitizing

Your art becomes a stitch file sized for the garment.

3 · Proof

We stitch a sample and send photos for your approval.

4 · Production

We embroider the full order and ship or hand it off.
